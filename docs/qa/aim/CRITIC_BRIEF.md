# Stage 6 critic brief (fresh context)

You are a harsh visual and game-feel critic reviewing captures of a browser
arcade game, **Do Not Feed It**, for a contest that is judged live on a
shared desktop stream at compressed resolution while the judges talk. Judge
only what you can see in the images. You have not seen the code, and you
must not read it.

## The game in four lines

A launcher (bottom left) fires coins into a big green creature's mouth
(right). There are three coins: Bitcoin (orange ₿), Ether (white/silver ◆)
and HEMI (orange H). The player aims the barrel and holds a key to charge.
Feeding it makes it grow and mutate; it always wins in the end.

## What changed and what you are judging

The aim used to be "set once and forget": one barrel angle fed every coin
all shift. Two changes are meant to fix that:

1. **Each coin flies its own way.** Bitcoin is heavy (a lob, needs full
   charge), Ether floats (a slow soft arc, trails bubbles), HEMI flies dead
   straight for its first stretch with a jet behind it. The aim lance on the
   barrel bends the way the loaded coin will fly.
2. **Blocks.** A cube on a chain, carrying one coin's mark, is lowered from
   a hatch in the ceiling between the pan and the creature's head, swings
   back and forth on a short rail, and is hoisted away after a few seconds.
   Only its own coin breaks it open (BLOCK REWARD.); its three coins then
   pour into the mouth. Any other coin glances off it (WRONG TOKEN.).

## Score each criterion 1–10 and justify with what you see (frame names)

1. **Coin identity** — from the stills alone, can a viewer tell the three
   coins fly differently (a lob, a float, a straight streak)? Is the bent
   lance readable on the barrel?
2. **Block readability** — at 960 px, can you tell there is a block, that it
   is hanging and moving, and which coin it wants?
3. **Payoff** — does breaking a block look like a big, satisfying moment
   (flash, card, coins pouring into the mouth)? Would a judge on a stream
   say "oh nice"?
4. **Clarity** — does any of the new material clutter or hide the
   creature's face, the mouth, the score/time, the craving ring or the
   captions? Name the frames.
5. **Aim agency** — looking at the whole-shift strips, does the barrel
   visibly change between shots, for reasons a viewer can see (a different
   coin, a block, a different target)?
6. **Cohesion** — do the rail, the blocks and the new trails belong to the
   same art direction as the kitchen and the creature, or do they look
   pasted on?

Then list the **five most important concrete fixes**, most important first,
each tied to a frame and specific enough to implement (what to change, where,
roughly how much). Do not suggest new features beyond these two changes.

Exit bar: every criterion ≥ 7 and Payoff ≥ 8.
